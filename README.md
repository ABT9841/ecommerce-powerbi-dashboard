# E-Commerce Analytics Dashboard | Power BI

## Project Overview

Built an interactive Power BI dashboard using the Olist E-Commerce dataset to analyze sales performance, customer behavior, product trends, delivery efficiency, and customer satisfaction across approximately 99K orders and $15.8M in revenue.

The objective was to transform raw transactional data into actionable business insights that support strategic decision-making.

---

## Dataset Overview

* Period Covered: September 2016 – September 2018
* Total Revenue: $15.84M
* Total Orders: 98,666
* Unique Customers: 96K+
* Average Order Value: $160.58
* Average Review Score: 4.03 / 5
* Average Delivery Time: 12.41 Days
* Cancellation Rate: 0.47%

**Note:** The dataset begins in September 2016 and ends in September 2018, resulting in partial-year data for 2016 and 2018. Therefore, Month-over-Month (MoM) analysis was used instead of traditional Year-over-Year (YoY) comparisons.

---

## Business Questions

This dashboard was designed to answer:

* Is revenue growing over time?
* Which categories generate the most revenue?
* Which states and cities drive sales?
* How strong is customer retention?
* Does delivery speed impact customer satisfaction?
* Which categories receive the highest and lowest reviews?
* What payment methods dominate customer purchases?
* Which operational areas present improvement opportunities?

---

## Key Insights

### Revenue Performance

* Revenue increased consistently between 2016 and 2018.
* November 2017 generated the highest monthly revenue at approximately $1.18M.
* Revenue reached peak performance during early 2018.
* Q2 delivered the strongest quarterly revenue performance.

**Business Recommendation:**
Prepare inventory and promotional campaigns before Q4 to capitalize on seasonal demand spikes.

---

### Product Performance

* Health & Beauty generated the highest revenue contribution at 9.1% of total revenue.
* Watches & Gifts achieved the highest average order value among top-performing categories.
* Bed Bath & Table generated the highest number of orders.
* Computer-related categories generated strong revenue despite lower order volumes, indicating premium pricing.
* Security & Services received the lowest review score (2.5).

**Business Recommendation:**
Focus on expanding top-performing categories while investigating customer satisfaction issues within underperforming categories.

---

### Customer Insights

* Approximately 93K customers were one-time buyers.
* Only 3K customers were repeat buyers.
* Repeat customers represented just 3.03% of the customer base.
* One-time buyers generated approximately $14.9M of total revenue.
* São Paulo dominated both customer concentration and revenue generation.

**Business Recommendation:**
Implement retention strategies such as loyalty programs, second-purchase discounts, and personalized recommendations to improve repeat purchase rates.

---

### Delivery & Customer Experience

* Average delivery time was 12.41 days.
* Customers receiving deliveries within 10 days generally provided higher review scores.
* Deliveries exceeding 20 days experienced significantly lower ratings.
* States with longer delivery times demonstrated weaker customer satisfaction.

**Business Recommendation:**
Improve logistics performance in slower regions to increase customer satisfaction and reduce negative reviews.

---

### Customer Satisfaction

* Average review score remained strong at 4.03/5.
* Faster delivery consistently correlated with better customer ratings.
* Top-performing sellers generally maintained stronger review scores.
* Certain categories generated strong sales but weaker customer satisfaction.

**Business Recommendation:**
Monitor delivery performance alongside review metrics to proactively identify customer experience issues.

---

### Payment Insights

* Credit Card was the most commonly used payment method.
* Cancellation rate remained below 1%, indicating strong operational stability.

**Business Recommendation:**
Continue optimizing payment experience while monitoring payment behavior trends across customer segments.

## Business Conclusion

The business demonstrated strong revenue growth and operational performance; however, customer retention remains the largest strategic challenge.

While revenue exceeded $15.8M and customer satisfaction remained high, only 3% of customers returned for additional purchases. Future growth may depend less on acquiring new customers and more on improving customer retention through loyalty programs, personalized marketing, and post-purchase engagement strategies.
